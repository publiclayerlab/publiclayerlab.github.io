Public Layer Lab can be contacted by email:

**publiclayerlab@gmail.com**

You are welcome to get in touch if you:

- have a question about the work;
- want to suggest or correct a source;
- know of a digital or administrative arrangement that may merit examination;
- work with or research a relevant system and would like to exchange perspectives;
- want to correct something Public Layer Lab has published;
- have an accessibility or privacy concern about this website.

Public Layer Lab is particularly interested in exchanges that can improve the factual basis, institutional understanding, or practical relevance of its work.

Contact does not imply a consultancy, legal, compliance, certification, or technical-audit engagement. Where a question requires specialist expertise outside Public Layer Lab's competence, that boundary will be kept explicit.

Public Layer Lab is the public-facing name of **Stichting Instituut voor Digitale Publieke Systemen**.

If you contact Public Layer Lab by email, the information you provide will be used to manage and respond to your message. More information is available on the [Privacy](/privacy/) page.
